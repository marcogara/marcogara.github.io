Singles beyond swipes berlin

A small, in-person community for singles in Berlin who want something real — less swiping, more talking.

What this is

This group is for single people who are serious about finding a partner and feel that dating apps have taken the human part out of dating. We meet in person occasionally — not too often, but regularly enough — to talk about ourselves, relationships, love, and what we want (and don’t).

It’s a safe, low-pressure space to get honest, learn from others, and improve the quality of your dating life.

How it works

  • We meet in relaxed in-person gatherings to share stories and perspectives — no dating pressure.
  • Talk about relationship wishes, boundaries, deal-breakers, and dating challenges.
  • Get gentle feedback from others (different genders and views) to broaden your perspective.
  • All genders and orientations welcome. Not for people already in a relationship or groups identifying as poly.

Why this?

Digital apps make dating fast and reachable, but they often remove the messy, human bits that lead to real connection. This is a chance to slow down, talk openly, and build better ways to meet people.

Rules

These are starter rules — we’ll refine them together as the group grows.

  1. Respect: Be kind and listen. No harassment or abusive behavior.
  2. Consent & privacy: Don’t share personal details or contact info outside the group without permission.
  3. No pitching: This is not a space for dating coaching sales, recruitment, or promoting services.
  4. No pressure: Meetings are low-pressure — attendance is voluntary and you don’t need to pursue anyone you meet here.
  5. Single only: Please join only if you identify as single and are exploring dating.
  6. Feedback kindly: Offer constructive, thoughtful feedback — not judgments.

Made by members for members — if you want to help set things up, say so when you join. ❤️
